Haoran (Ryan) Xu 徐浩然

Ph.D. student/The University of Texas at Austin

I am a 4th‑year Ph.D. student at UT Austin, advised by Prof. Amy Zhang, and supported by the Amazon AI PhD Fellowship.

My research asks how to pretrain foundation models with reinforcement learning, and explores the new post-training paradigm that pretraining brings. The aim is to keep pretraining stable and scalable to large models, while learning from more diverse data, including sub‑optimal data, to unlock capabilities beyond imitation.

I am open to collaboration; feel free to reach out.
